## Pilot
The [[Pilot program]]: baseline, security plan, [[Record Keeper]] and one more role (usually the [[Briefing Bot]]), weekly check-ins, and a readout with a go / no-go. The pilot fee may be credited toward a tier if the agreement says so.

## Starter
For a first bot team of one or two.
- Roles: [[Record Keeper]] + one of [[Briefing Bot]], [[Channel Liaison]], or [[Idea Partner]].
- Routines: daily review, weekly review, and one scheduled report or one event watch.
- Skills: the starter set in Skills/, adapted and tested on two days' inputs.
- Connectors: up to three (for example the vault repo, email, chat).
- Support: {{PROVIDER_CONTACT}}, response time as in [[Service scope]].

## Team
The full starter team, launched one bot at a time over 6 to 8 weeks.
- Roles: [[Record Keeper]], [[Briefing Bot]], [[Channel Liaison]], [[Idea Partner]], then [[Coordinator]].
- Routines: everything in Starter, plus the Coordinator's morning status and weekly team review, the liaison's channel watch, and the weekly idea triage.
- Team setup: roster and lanes in [[Bot configuration]], hand-off format, shared [[Bot playbook]] lessons.
- Connectors: as discovery calls for, least access.

## Managed
The provider runs the team for the client month to month.
- Everything in Team.
- Monthly report and access review, prompt tuning from the weekly grades, pausing ignored routines, usage-pace watch, lessons kept in the [[Bot playbook]].
- New skills and routines within the included change requests.
- Quarterly review against the pilot baseline.
