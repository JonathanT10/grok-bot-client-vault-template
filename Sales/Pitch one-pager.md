# An assistant team for your office that writes everything down

**{{PROVIDER_NAME}}** sets up Grok Bot for small businesses: a small team of AI assistants, each with one clear job, plus a private record of everything they do. You stay in charge of every decision.

## The problem
Small teams lose hours every week to the same jobs: sorting email, chasing follow-ups, pulling together updates, and remembering who decided what. AI chat tools can help, but on their own they forget, drift, and leave nothing you can check later. Setting one up properly takes time most owners do not have.

## What we set up
- **Your own record, not chat memory.** A private, git-backed notes vault that you own and can open in Obsidian or a web browser. Decisions, changes, reports, and the setup itself are written down there.
- **Assistants with clear jobs.** Start with a **Record Keeper**, then add roles as they earn their place: **Coordinator**, **Briefing Bot**, **Channel Liaison**, **Idea Partner**, and **Content Producer**.
- **Ready-made skills and routines.** Daily and weekly reviews, inbox triage, research briefs, meeting prep and follow-up, idea triage, a decision board, and a monthly report, adapted to how you work.
- **Guardrails built in.** You approve anything that sends, spends, publishes, deletes, changes permissions, or connects an account. Passwords stay in your password manager, never in the notes.

## How it works
1. **Discovery.** One conversation about your business, your tools, and the three jobs that would help most. We write down how long they take today.
2. **Setup.** We build your vault, connect only the tools you need (you sign in yourself), and launch one assistant at a time.
3. **Gradual handover.** Week 1 the assistants only draft. Week 2 you OK each action. Week 3 routine cases run on their own. Week 4 they run on schedule and you read a weekly summary.
4. **Go-live and support.** We sign off a go-live checklist together, every line with proof. Then we keep things tuned with check-ins, a monthly report, and support.
