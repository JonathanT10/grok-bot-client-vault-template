## 4. Included in every package
- **You own the vault.** It lives in your private repository. At the end of service it stays with you, and our access and the assistants' access are removed.
- **You approve the big things.** Anything that sends, spends, publishes, deletes, changes permissions, or connects or disconnects an account waits for you.
- **Draft-only first weeks** under the gradual handover, then more independence only as the work earns it.
- **No passwords in the notes.** Credentials stay in your password manager; the vault records only where each one lives.
- **Proof, not promises.** Reports show where each number came from and end with a list of decisions waiting on you.

## 5. Not included
- Your Grok Bot plan and usage, and any subscriptions for the tools we connect. These are billed to you directly.
- Running your IT, or holding your passwords.
- Legal, tax, or financial advice.
- Sending, spending, or publishing on your behalf without your approval.
- Work beyond your included change requests, roles, routines, or connectors (see add-ons).

## 6. Pilot terms
- **Length:** 30 days, with dates and a readout date set at kickoff.
- **Includes:** discovery, a baseline and security plan, your vault, the Record Keeper and one use-case assistant, weekly check-ins, a written readout, and a go / no-go meeting.
- **You provide:** a sponsor who approves actions, about one week of time logging at the start and end, admin sign-in for the tools we connect, and the AI vendor's data terms for your plan in writing.
- **During the pilot:** assistants research, draft, and record. People send, post, and change systems.
- **Outcomes:** go (choose a package above), extend once by up to 30 days with a named fix, or stop. If you stop, assistant and provider access is removed and the vault stays yours.
