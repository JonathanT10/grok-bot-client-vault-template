# Meeting prep and follow-up

Date: {{START_DATE}}
Tags: #skill #meetings
Status: starter (adapt and test on two days before trusting)

Before a meeting: a one-screen brief. After: notes, decisions, and action items in the vault, with follow-up drafts for the approver to send.

## When to use
- Prep: on request, or by a routine that looks at tomorrow's calendar.
- Follow-up: after the meeting, from notes, a transcript, or what the client tells you.

## Inputs
- The calendar event (title, time, attendees).
- Vault notes on the attendees ([[People and roles]], People/), related Projects/, open follow-ups in Decisions/.
- For follow-up: the notes or transcript.

## Steps
### Prep
1. Pull the vault. Create a meeting note from the [[Meeting note template]] in the related project, or in Daily/ if none.
2. Fill Prep: who is attending (roles), last interactions found in the vault, open items, 3 suggested questions.
3. Send the brief to chat. Log one line.

### Follow-up
1. Fill Notes, Decisions, and Action items (owner, due date) from the source. Do not invent anything that was not said.
2. Big or hard-to-undo decisions get their own Decisions/ record.
3. Draft a follow-up email; it waits for an approver to send.
4. Log one line, commit.

## Done when
- Success: prep reads in one minute; every action item has an owner and a date; nothing is sent without approval.
- Failure: invented attendees or decisions, action items with no owner, or private details recorded beyond the privacy rules in [[GROK]].
