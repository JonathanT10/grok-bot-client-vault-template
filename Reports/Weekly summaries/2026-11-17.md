## Guardrails to keep

- On the runbook, the bot and the provider draft and check. Every live change (directory, identity, device, DNS, DHCP, network, file share, application, certificate, backup, or power state) needs written approval from `{{PRIMARY_CONTACT}}` for that action, in the form ACTION / TARGET / REASON / COST / CONSEQUENCES. A missing gate answer is a no. If break-glass (R8) or backups (R9) is "no" or "unknown", do not start Stage 1. The bot may draft staff messages. A named person sends them after approval.
- On incidents, follow `Skills/Incident response.md` and Guardrails section 11. The bot does not notify regulators, customers, insurers, or the press. The decision aid is a checklist. It states no notice deadlines. Leave a window blank until counsel, the contract, or the policy names it, and record the source.
- The data handling notice is a plain overview for the client owner. Binding terms stay in the signed agreement and `Reference/Guardrails policy.md`. Retention rows stay blank until the client's policy and current vendor docs are confirmed.
- Standing rules in `README.md` and `GROK.md` still apply. No secrets in the vault. Client data stays in that client's vault. A person approves anything that sends, spends, publishes, deletes, changes permissions, or connects or disconnects an account.

## How to use this week's additions during setup

- Use the runbook when the client is moving identity and devices to Entra ID and Intune. Start with the readiness checks and a read-only inventory. Stages, in order: 0 safety net and look-around, 1 pilot, 2 broaden, 3 cut directory sync, 4 soft shutdown and rollback window, 5 final decommission. Start a stage only after its gate is a written yes. Record the chosen end state (soft shutdown only, or full decommission) under `Decisions/` from `Templates/Decision record template.md`.
- Use the data handling notice in the first onboarding conversations so the client sees what the bot can see and where notes live.
- Use the incident response skill and the decision aid before go-live so staff have one path: Stop, Report, Contain, Assess, Fix and record. Outside notice stays a human decision.
- Provider demos stay on the fictional demo vault. The demo script is about 10 minutes. Delete `Business/` and `Sales/` from every client copy.
