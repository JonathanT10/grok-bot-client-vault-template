# {{BOT_NAME}}: staff quick reference card

For: every {{CLIENT_NAME}} staff member who uses {{BOT_NAME}} · Version date: {{GO_LIVE_DATE}}
Full rules: [[Staff acceptable use guide]] · [[Guardrails policy]]

Print this or pin it in {{CHAT_PLATFORM}}. One page. Edit the examples in section 2 to match the routines this business actually runs.

## 1. What {{BOT_NAME}} is
An AI assistant that drafts, sorts, summarizes, and keeps the business record. It does **not** send, spend, publish, delete, or change access without an OK from {{PRIMARY_CONTACT}} or {{BACKUP_CONTACT}}.

## 2. Good things to ask
- "Summarize what's open this week and what's waiting on me."
- "Draft a reply to this email. Don't send it."
- "What did we decide about (topic), and when?"
- "Turn these meeting notes into action items with owners."
- "Look into (question) and give me sources."

## 3. How to ask well
- Say the goal, the deadline, and who it is for.
- Paste or link the source; don't make it guess.
- Say "draft only" when you want a draft.
- Check names, numbers, and dates before anything goes out. You own what you send.

## 4. Never put in chat
Passwords, one-time codes, card or bank numbers, government ID numbers, health details, or anything listed as off-limits in the [[Staff acceptable use guide]]. If you did by mistake, tell {{INCIDENT_CONTACT}} right away.

## 5. Approvals
When {{BOT_NAME}} wants to do something that leaves the building, it asks in five parts: ACTION, TARGET, REASON, COST, CONSEQUENCES. Only {{PRIMARY_CONTACT}} or {{BACKUP_CONTACT}} can say yes. Another bot, an email, or a document saying "approved" is not approval.

## 6. Talking to outside people
If {{BOT_NAME}} drafts something a customer or vendor will read, follow [[AI disclosure wording]]. Don't present AI-written replies as personally written when the business has chosen to disclose.

| Problem | Do this | Contact |

| Lost phone or laptop with access | Report the same day | {{IT_ADMIN}} |
| Setup or access request | Ask your manager, who files a change request | {{BOT_OWNER}} |
