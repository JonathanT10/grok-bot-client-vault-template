- **Est. min/wk saved** = minutes per week today x the share a draft-only routine can take off (be conservative) - expected review minutes per week. Label the figure with its evidence type; a bot estimate stays a bot estimate until the time log replaces it.
- **Access needed:** system, scope (read / draft), and who grants it. "Edit", "send", or "approve" in a source system is out of scope for this pilot.

## Scoring rubric
Score = **Value + Evidence + (4 - Complexity) + (4 - Risk) + Access ease**, maximum 15. Rank by score; break ties by higher measured minutes, then lower risk.

| Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Value** (est. min/wk saved) | under 30 | 30-120 | over 120 |
| **Evidence** (source of the time figure) | bot estimate | owner estimate | measured, with an evidence path |
| **Complexity** (subtracted) | one source, fixed format, clear rules | two or three sources or some judgment | many sources, free-form judgment, or exceptions most days |
| **Risk** (subtracted) | FYI only; a wrong output costs a minute to spot | drafts to staff or vendors; a person reviews every one | money, legal, customer-facing, or sensitive data in the path |
| **Access ease** | new connector with admin consent | an existing connector, new scope | read access already granted |

Rules:
- **Risk 3 tasks are not piloted**, whatever the score; list them for later with what would make them safe.
- A task whose only evidence is a bot estimate cannot rank above a task with measured evidence and a similar score; get a time-log figure first.
- Re-score after the first week of the time log if any figure moved by more than 25%.
