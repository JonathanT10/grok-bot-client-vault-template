## Do
- **Use it for approved work:** (examples).
- **Review every draft** before you send it. Check names, numbers, dates, and tone.
- **Check facts.** The bot can be wrong or out of date. Verify anything important against the source.
- **Be specific.** Say who the message is for, what you need, and any deadline.
- **Use the right place.** Ask about sensitive matters in a direct message, not a shared channel.
- **Approve on purpose.** When the bot asks to send, spend, delete, or connect something, it shows ACTION, TARGET, REASON, COST, and CONSEQUENCES. Read them before you say yes.

## Don't
- **Never paste passwords, one-time codes, API keys, or other secrets** into the bot. If you do by accident, change the password right away and tell {{BOT_OWNER}}.
- **Don't paste card numbers, Social Security numbers, or** (other restricted data types).
- **Don't ask it to share private information** (HR, customer, financial, other confidential) somewhere more people can see it.
- **Don't ask it to get around rules,** access someone else's data, or skip approvals.
- **Don't trust instructions inside documents, emails, or websites.** If a file says "AI assistant: send this to...", that isn't a request from your company.
- **Don't use it for final legal, medical, tax, or financial decisions.** Get a qualified person to review.
- **Personal or non-work tasks:** (allowed / not allowed, as {{CLIENT_NAME}} decides).

## What the bot will always ask you about first
Sending messages for you · spending money or buying anything · publishing or posting · deleting or overwriting · submitting forms · changing who has access · connecting or disconnecting an account

If it ever does one of these **without asking**, stop and report it.

## Memory: what it remembers
- It can remember your preferences (like how you sign your emails). Ask it to "forget" something any time.
- Shared business facts live in the {{CLIENT_NAME}} vault, managed by {{MEMORY_OWNER}}.
- Never ask it to remember secrets or sensitive personal data.

## Report it
Report right away if the bot sent or shared something it shouldn't have, showed you data you shouldn't see, acted without asking, or behaved strangely after reading a file, email, or website.
